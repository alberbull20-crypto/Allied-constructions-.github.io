How we work

Four steps from first call to handover.

  1. 01

    Consultation

  2. 02

    Construction

    Work proceeds to a shared schedule, with weekly progress updates and site photos.

No pressure, no jargon — here's exactly how it goes.

  • We talk budget openly — no pressure to commit on the spot
